    II

    Where the record goes

    Every lender starts the same business from zero.

    A shop with eight years of invoices, a supplier who has never been paid late, and two loans closed on time. None of it leaves the place it happened in. The next funder is handed a form.

    Invoices

    Eight years of them, in an accounting file nobody outside the business can read.

    Supplier terms

    A reputation held in one person's memory. It does not survive them changing jobs.

    Two closed loans

    Repaid in full, on time. Known only to the lender that issued them.

    Bank statements

    A snapshot of one month, read as if it were a history.

    Tax standing

    Proof of compliance, which is not the same thing as proof of reliability.

    Who the business is

    Re-proved from scratch at every institution, from the same documents, by hand.

    Six true things about one business, and no way to hand them to the next funder.

    III

    The Business Passport

    One document, with the fields a funder actually asks for.

    Fondealo issues the Passport. The business holds it. Because the shape is fixed, a funder reads the same eight fields every time, whoever issued the credential and whatever the business sells.

    • Non transferable.The credential is bound to the business that earned it. It cannot be sold or lent.
    • Personal data stays off chain.Verification is recorded as a KYB status. Only a hash is written to Soroban, and the documents never leave the places that already hold them.
    • Read by anyone, written by one thing.Any funder can verify the Passport. Only loan_escrow can report a repayment to credit_score.

    It takes 0 contracts.

    business_passport issues the credential. credit_score holds the reputation. loan_escrow moves the USDC and is the only reporter the score accepts.

    Reputation that evolves

    Nothing new was created. The same six signals, read in one shape.

    Every repayment is reported by loan_escrow and recorded by credit_score. The escrow writes the record, not the borrower.

    Bound to the business. Carried to the next funder. Added to, never restarted.

    Design intent: the continuous score update on repayment is not live in production today. The contracts that do it are written and deploy to Testnet.

    V

    Opportunities and the ecosystem

    Two sides, one document between them.

    The business

    Publishes an opportunity: the amount, the term, and what the money is for. The Passport travels with the request, so the funder is reading fields instead of forming an impression.

    Repayments go back through the escrow, which is what makes them count toward the reputation.

    The funder

    Reads the Passport, funds in USDC, and is repaid through loan_escrow. Because the escrow is the only thing that can report a repayment, the record cannot be flattered by either side.

    The same credential is readable by the next funder, which is the part a private credit file has never done.

    VI

    The future of business credit

    Credit in Latin America is still written from scratch for every lender. It does not have to be. A business that has paid on time should be able to prove it to the next funder without asking the last one for permission. That is the whole of Fondealo: one document, owned by the business, read the same way by everyone, and longer every year. Start a Passport.
